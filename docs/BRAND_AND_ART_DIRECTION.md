# Brand & Art Direction

## Desired perception
The site should feel:
- premium
- editorial
- feminine without cliché
- sophisticated
- contemporary
- intimate
- confident
- tactile
- internationally credible

## Creative principle
Think **luxury beauty editorial + modern digital magazine + personal authority**, not “influencer template.”

## Visual language
Explore:
- refined typography
- generous negative space
- macro beauty imagery
- tactile textures
- elegant editorial grids
- sophisticated transitions
- controlled motion
- asymmetric compositions
- cinematic media treatment

## Avoid
- generic pink beauty templates
- excessive rounded cards
- random gradients
- excessive glassmorphism
- noisy particle effects
- gratuitous 3D
- overuse of shadows
- animation everywhere
- stock photography as the primary identity

## Photography direction
Prioritize:
- authentic Zina imagery
- product texture
- skin/detail photography
- controlled studio lighting
- editorial portraits
- behind-the-scenes testing
- campaign work

Never fabricate or synthetically imply a real collaboration or product test.

## Motion
Motion should:
- establish hierarchy
- reveal content
- create continuity
- reinforce premium feel

Motion must respect:
`prefers-reduced-motion`

Never make core information dependent on animation.

## Mobile
Mobile is not a compressed desktop. The art direction must be designed for touch, readability, performance, and one-handed navigation.
