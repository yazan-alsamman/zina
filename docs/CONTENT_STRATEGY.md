# Content Strategy

## Editorial positioning
The site should not read like a resume. It should feel like a curated editorial destination built around Zina's real beauty-testing work.

## Core content types

### 1. Review
Purpose: capture high-intent product searches and demonstrate expertise.

Required fields:
- title
- slug
- product
- brand
- category
- hero media
- summary
- testing context
- observations
- results
- pros
- cons
- suitability
- verdict
- disclosure
- date
- related content

### 2. Brand
Purpose: showcase verified relationships and relevant work.

### 3. Work / Collaboration
Purpose: demonstrate professional capability to brands and agencies.

### 4. Journal
Purpose: capture broader informational searches and deepen topical authority.

### 5. About
Purpose: identity, philosophy, approach, credibility.

## Editorial principles
- First-hand experience should be obvious.
- Separate observation from fact.
- Clearly distinguish sponsored/collaborative content.
- Never make medical or clinical claims without appropriate evidence and framing.
- Avoid absolute claims such as “best for everyone.”
- Keep reviews balanced.
- Update evergreen content when meaningful changes occur.

## Content production workflow
1. Topic selection
2. Search-intent research
3. Evidence/product verification
4. First-hand testing
5. Draft
6. Fact check
7. SEO optimization
8. Editorial review
9. Media optimization
10. Publish
11. Internal-link pass
12. Search Console monitoring

## Content backlog
Claude Code should create a content inventory document with:
- confirmed existing content
- missing content
- high-priority pages
- future ideas
- required assets
- verification status

Do not invent the backlog from assumptions. Use confirmed information.
