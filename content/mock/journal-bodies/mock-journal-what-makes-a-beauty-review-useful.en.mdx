## The three things a review must separate

What the brand claims, what the reviewer observed, what the reviewer concluded. Blur these and the review becomes advertising.

## Conditions are not a detail

The environment a product was tested in is part of the result, not context around it.

## Why suits everyone is a warning sign

A review that cannot name who a product does not suit has not finished testing it.

## Disclosure at the top, not the bottom

Where the disclosure sits tells you what the reviewer thinks disclosure is for.

## The update is part of the review

An undated review of a reformulated product is misinformation with good intentions.

## What a score hides

A number makes comparison easy and flattens the reason. Why this site does not publish one.
